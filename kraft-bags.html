Sizing guide

How to measure your bag.

Every bag size is listed as length x width x height, in inches.

  1. 1

    Length

    Across the front face of the bag.

  2. 2

    Width

    The side gusset, or how deep the bag is.

  3. 3

    Height

    From the bottom to the top edge. Handles are not included.
